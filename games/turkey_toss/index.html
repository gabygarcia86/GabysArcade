How to Play

  • Press the slice, drag up, release. Drag direction aims and drag length sets the distance.
  • Land slices on plates. Farther plates are worth more: 1, 2, 3, 5, and 10 for the back plate.
  • Fill all nine plates for a permanent 4X.
  • Cranberry bowl: each hit gives 2X for 10 seconds.
  • Gravy bowl: each hit gives 3X for 10 seconds.
  • Giant turkey: bounce off it and land on a plate for 10X on that landing.
  • Cornucopia: hit it for 2X for 10 seconds, and a shower of fruit.
  • Flying turkey: it crosses once per round. Hit it for +100.
  • Watch the edges: a slice on the floor costs 10 points.

Multipliers stack by multiplying. You have 60 seconds and 60 slices.
