    HOW TO PLAY

    Three laps, four racers. Drive through every checkpoint gate in order. Cutting across doesn't count.

    CONTROLS

    W / ↑
    Accelerate
    S / ↓
    Brake, then reverse
    A D / ← →
    Steer (tap for small corrections)
    Space / Shift
    Boost (uses the boost tank)
    R
    Back on track if you're stuck
    Esc / P
    Pause
    Gamepad
    Stick steers, RT/A accelerate, LT/B brake, X/RB boost
    Touch
    Stick (or ◀ ▶) on the left; ACCEL, BRAKE, BOOST on the right (BOOST also accelerates)

    TECHNIQUE

    • Turning is tightest at low and medium speed. Flat out, the turning circle widens, so brake before tight corners.
    • Power-slide: hold brake while steering at speed. The tail swings out and you carve tighter.
    • Drift boost: hold a slide until the sparks turn blue, orange, then purple. Let go to cash it in for boost energy and a burst of speed.
    • Boost recharges when you're not using it. Blue chevron pads give a free kick.
    • Rocket start: hit the throttle while the "1" is showing. Too early and the engine overheats.
    • Off the track (sand, snow, lava…) you lose speed. Lava is worst: you'll be lifted out, slowly.

    HAZARDS

    • Probe-Fighters patrol sections of track. Their red scanning beam shows where they're heading.
    • Shock gates crackle before they fire, and only ever block half the track. Lava vents glow before erupting. Falling rocks cast a growing shadow. Asteroids are announced by blinking chevrons.
